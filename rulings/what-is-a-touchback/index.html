You Asked

What is a touchback, and where does the ball go?

Tito Interpretation

A touchback is a ball that is dead on or behind the goal line a team is defending, where the impetus that sent it there came from an opponent, and it is not a touchdown or an incomplete forward pass. Where the ball comes out depends on how it got there. The standard spot is the 20-yard line: a punt downed in the end zone, an interception or a recovery in the end zone, a ball out the back of it. Kickoffs carry a second answer. If a kickoff touches the ground or a player in the landing zone and is then downed in the end zone or goes out of bounds behind the goal line, the ball comes out to the 20. If the kickoff reaches the end zone without first touching the ground or a player in the landing zone — downed there, out the back, or off the goal post — the ball comes out to the 35. And on a kickoff from the 50, a touchback is spotted at the 20 either way.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Half the internet still answers 25, and under the book as it stands no touchback is spotted at the 25: it is the 20, or the 35 on the untouched kickoff. The argument in most living rooms is one bounce — a kickoff that lands in the landing zone first and then trickles into the end zone is a 20, not a 35, because the landing-zone touch is what separates the two spots.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 11, Section 6, Article 1 defines a touchback as a ball dead on or behind the goal line a team is defending, provided that the impetus comes from an opponent, and that it is not a touchdown or an incomplete forward pass. Article 3 places the ball next in play at the 20-yard line, or, if the touchback results from a free kick, at the 35-yard line or the 20-yard line as applicable under Rule 6, Section 1, Article 5. That article sets the dead ball spot at the 20 when a kickoff or safety kick touches the ground or a player in the landing zone and is downed in the end zone or goes out of bounds behind the goal line, and at the 35 when, without first touching the ground or a player in the landing zone, it goes out of bounds behind the goal line, strikes the goal post, uprights, or crossbar, or lands at or beyond the goal line and is downed in the end zone — and at the 20 in either case when the kickoff is from the 50-yard line.
