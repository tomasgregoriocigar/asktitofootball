You Asked

When can a player return to the game after going to the medical tent?

Tito Interpretation

The book's rule is about the injury timeout, not the tent: when play is stopped for an injured player, he must leave the game for the completion of one down before he can return. He may stay in without sitting out if either team calls a charged timeout, if his injury came from an opponent's foul, or if the period ends or the two-minute warning intervenes. Whether he is cleared to play again after a head injury check is a medical decision under the league's concussion protocol, not something the rulebook decides.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Fans think the tent works like a pit stop, in and straight back out. The book makes him miss at least one play, and the exceptions are the part nobody knows: a charged timeout called by either team wipes the sit-out entirely.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 4, Section 5, Article 3 provides that when an injury timeout is called, the injured player must leave the game for the completion of one down. The player is permitted to remain in the game if either team calls a charged team timeout, if the injury is the result of a foul by an opponent, or if the period ends or the two-minute warning occurs before the next snap.
