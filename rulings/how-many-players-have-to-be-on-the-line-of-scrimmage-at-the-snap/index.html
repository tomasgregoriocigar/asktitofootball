You Asked

How many players have to be on the line of scrimmage at the snap?

Tito Interpretation

The offense must have at least seven players on the line of scrimmage at the snap. Eligible receivers have to be on both ends of that line, and every player between them must be an ineligible receiver. Breaking this is illegal formation, a 5-yard penalty.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Fans often think the offense can line up any way it likes before the snap, but the book sets the minimum at seven on the line, with eligible players only at the ends. Shifting the formation does not change the requirement at the snap.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 7, Section 5, Article 1 requires the offensive team, at the snap, to have seven or more players on the line, with eligible receivers on both ends of the line and all of the players on the line between them ineligible receivers. The penalty for illegal formation by the offense is loss of five yards.
