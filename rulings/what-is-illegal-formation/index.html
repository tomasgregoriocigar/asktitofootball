You Asked

What is illegal formation?

Tito Interpretation

Illegal formation is the offense failing Rule 7, Section 5's checklist at the snap, and it costs five yards. The offense must have seven or more players on the line of scrimmage. Eligible receivers must be the players on both ends of the line, and every player on the line between them must be an ineligible receiver — which is why an eligible receiver lined up on the line inside the end man is covered up and cannot legally catch a pass. No player may be out of bounds, and players in eligible positions must wear eligible numbers unless they have reported to the Referee in accordance with Rule 5, Section 3, Article 1.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Most fans know only the count: not enough men on the line. That is item (a) of the article. The calls that confuse everyone are the other lines of the checklist — the covered receiver, a wideout standing on the line one step inside the outside man and ineligible without knowing it, and the tackle-eligible who never reported. Same article, same five yards.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 7, Section 5, Article 1 requires the offensive team, at the snap, to have seven or more players on the line; to have eligible receivers on both ends of the line, with all of the players on the line between them ineligible receivers; to have no player out of bounds; and to have players in eligible positions wearing eligible numbers, unless they have reported in accordance with Rule 5, Section 3, Article 1. The penalty for illegal formation by the offense is a loss of five yards.
