You Asked

Is the tush push legal?

Tito Interpretation

Yes. Teammates may push the ball carrier from behind, and the book does not prohibit it. What the book prohibits under assisting the runner is pulling a runner in any direction at any time, using interlocking interference by grasping a teammate or encircling his body with the hands or arms to block an opponent, and pushing or throwing your body against a teammate to aid him in obstructing an opponent or recovering a loose ball. None of those is a shove on the runner himself. When assisting the runner is called, the penalty is 10 yards.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Fans hear the name of the foul, assisting the runner, and assume all help is banned. Read the list in the book: the banned acts are pulling the runner and pushing teammates who are blocking. Pushing the man with the ball is simply not on the list, which is why the play remains legal.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 12, Section 1, Article 4 provides that no offensive player may pull a runner in any direction at any time, use interlocking interference, or push or throw his body against a teammate to aid him in an attempt to obstruct an opponent or to recover a loose ball, and sets the penalty at a loss of 10 yards. Pushing the runner is not among the prohibited acts.
