You Asked

Was Fernando Mendoza really past the line of scrimmage?

Tito Interpretation

The book does not flag a toe. It flags the whole man. A forward pass is illegal only when the passer's entire body and the ball are beyond the line of scrimmage when the ball is released. On the last frame any angle gives us with the ball still in Mendoza's hands, his feet are straddling the line at the 10: front foot past it, back foot on it, ball still cocked to throw. That is not an entire body beyond the line. He was running forward, and no publicly available angle shows the release itself, so the honest limit is this: the flag erased a touchdown on a fact that the last visible evidence does not establish, and that replay cannot establish either.

Keep the stakes straight, too: the Raiders scored two plays later anyway, Mendoza to Jack Bech, and lost 38-24. This is not a stolen win. It is a standard the evidence never met.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Fans watch the ball and the front foot, and the blue line on the broadcast does the rest. The book watches the whole body. A toe past the line, a shoulder past the line, even the ball past the line is not enough on its own: the test is the entire body and the ball, together, beyond the line at the release. Fans also read the penalty as five yards and move on. The book's real bite is the loss of down, with the five yards walked off from the spot where the ball was released, not from the line of scrimmage.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 8, Section 1, Article 2 allows one forward pass from behind the line during each down, and Item 1(a) makes a forward pass illegal when it is thrown when the passer's entire body and the ball are beyond the line of scrimmage when the ball is released, whether the passer is airborne or touching the ground. The penalty for a forward pass from beyond the line is loss of down and five yards from the spot where the ball is released. The ruling is reviewable: Rule 15, Section 3, Article 6, Item 1 covers plays governed by the line of scrimmage, including whether a forward pass was thrown when the passer was beyond the line of scrimmage. But under Rule 15, Section 2, Article 1, an on-field ruling is changed only when clear and obvious video evidence warrants a change.

NFL Rulebook — Rule 8, Section 1, Article 2; Rule 15, Section 3, Article 6; Rule 15, Section 2, Article 1
