What is the 10-second runoff?

Tito's Ruling

Inside the final two minutes of a half, after the two-minute warning, certain clock-stopping events caused by the offense (a foul, an injury, a replay reversal that changes a ruling) can cost 10 seconds off the clock, and the game or half can end on it. The defense can decline the runoff for a foul or an injury, but not for a replay reversal; a timeout can buy the 10 seconds back.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

Fans think it is a penalty yardage issue; it is a time penalty, and time is the one thing you cannot get back.

NFL Rulebook — Rule 4, Section 5
