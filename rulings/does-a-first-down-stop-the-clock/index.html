You Asked

Does a first down stop the clock?

Tito's Ruling

Not in the NFL. The clock keeps running on a first down (it may pause for a measurement and restarts on the ready signal).

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

College and high school trained the instinct; the NFL never adopted the stop, and college itself now only stops inside the final 2 minutes of each half (NCAA, 2023).

NFL Rulebook — Rule 4; NCAA Rule 3 for the college contrast
