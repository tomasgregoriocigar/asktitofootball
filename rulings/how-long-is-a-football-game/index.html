You Asked

How long is a football game?

Tito Interpretation

Sixty minutes. Four periods of 15 minutes each, with a 13-minute halftime between the second and third periods. If the score is tied at the end of the fourth period, overtime extends the game.

Why Everyone Gets It Wrong

The game clock says 60 minutes, but a broadcast runs about three hours. The difference is everything that stops the clock: incomplete passes, runners out of bounds, timeouts, penalties, reviews, and the breaks between periods.

Rule Book Confirmed

Rule 4, Section 1, Article 1 fixes the length of the game at 60 minutes, divided into four periods of 15 minutes each. The same article provides that if the score is tied at the end of four periods, the game is extended by an overtime period or periods as prescribed in Rule 16.
